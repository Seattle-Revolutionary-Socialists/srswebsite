## Why We Need a Revolutionary Party
### Required Readings
### Study Questions

- What's the social democratic view of the relationship between party and class?
- How were Rosa Luxemburg's ideas different from the ideas of social democrats like Kautsky? How does she relate the "conservative" role a party can play to the "spontaneity" of the masses?
- How are Trotsky's ideas similar to Luxemburg's? What are his arguments in support of his distrust of centralized organization?
- How do Lenin and Gramsci's thought acknowledge the problems identified by Luxemburg and Trotsky but also move beyond them? What is the role of the party in relation to the working class?
- How is the Bolshevik (Leninist) conception of the party distinct from the social democratic and Stalinist conceptions?
- Why is it undesirable for the Leninist party to become the state after capitalism is overthrown? What is the role of the party to the masses during revolution?

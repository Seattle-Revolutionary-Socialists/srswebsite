### Study Questions

- What are some examples you can think of that show the alienation and division within the working class? What are some counter-examples?
- Why is the working class "the one class in society with a material interest in challenging all forms of oppression"?
- How does the working class get divided under capitalism? What stands in the way of the members of the working class recognizing their common interests and uniting?
- How are systems of oppression, such as white supremacy and patriarchy, related to the system of exploitation (capitalism)?
- What is the author's critique of privilege theory?
- Why do some members of the working class accept reactionary ideas, even if these ideas contradict their material interests? How can this dynamic be overcome?
- What is class struggle, and how does participating in the struggle lead to greater consciousness by the working class?
- Why is revolutionary organization so important for the class struggle?
